          How the numbers are calculated
          • DTG of Incident is read as a date: 02NOV26, 02 NOV 26, 02NOV2026, 02-Nov-26, Excel dates and dates such as 11/2/2026. Day of week, weekend vs weekday, holiday and payday windows come from it. If a DTG also carries a time (021430ZNOV26 or 11/2/2026 14:30), the time-of-day filter and heat map appear automatically; if no incident has a time, they stay hidden and nothing is inferred about hours. TY is shown as given; the federal fiscal year is always worked out from the DTG.
          • Fiscal year is the federal year, 1 October to 30 September (FY26 = Oct 2025 to Sep 2026). Fiscal month 1 = October. Quarters: Q1 Oct–Dec, Q2 Jan–Mar, Q3 Apr–Jun, Q4 Jul–Sep.
          • Unique people = distinct Person IDs. Repeat rate = people with 2 or more incidents in the selection, or any incident marked Repeat Incident = Y, divided by unique people.
          • % referred = incidents with Referred to Treatment = Y. % completed = Completed Program = Y among referred incidents.
          • Trend vs prior FY compares the latest (or selected) fiscal year with the year before over the same fiscal months, so a part year is compared fairly.
          • Rates per 1,000 need a headcount (file or typed). Rate = incidents ÷ assigned × 1,000 × 12 ÷ months in the period (annualized). Relative risk = a group's rate ÷ the overall rate. Rates only show when the headcount covers the current filters (for example a Status or Since-deployment filter has no headcount, so rankings fall back to shares).
          • Without a headcount, weak spots use the share of incidents, the repeat rate compared with the overall repeat rate, and (for timing) incidents per day compared with other days.
          • Holiday window = within 3 days of a federal holiday, New Year’s Eve or St. Patrick’s Day. Payday window = 0–3 days after the 1st or 15th. Their relative risk compares incidents per calendar day inside vs outside the window.
          • Small groups: counts are shown in full on the dashboard by default. “Hide small groups (<5)” shows any group under 5 as <5 on the map, charts, heat maps and summary. Rankings and recommendations always need 5+ incidents per group, for statistical stability. The Copy for GenAI pack and .txt always hide groups under 5, whatever the setting, because that text leaves the dashboard.
          • Privacy: name, ID, SSN and DoD ID / EDIPI columns are skipped without reading; DOB is read only when Age is blank and is not retained. Mailing addresses are reduced to town only. Blank, #REF! and other Excel error cells count as blank. Everything runs in this browser; no network requests.
          • Correlations: bias-corrected Cramér’s V from a chi-square test on each pair of fields (values under 5 pooled), needing 30+ incidents. Pairs that are related by design (unit and location, referred and completed, grade and age or service) are listed separately and not ranked.
          • Automated recommendations are generated from the data to start a discussion. They are not clinical or legal advice.
