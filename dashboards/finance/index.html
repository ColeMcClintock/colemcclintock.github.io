How the numbers work
  • Annual Funding Program (AFP): the total planned funding for the fiscal year for a program and organization.
  • Allotment (funds received): the portion of the AFP actually released so far. Releases come in quarterly increments, smaller during a continuing resolution.
  • Commitment: an administrative reservation of funds for a planned purchase (for example, an approved purchase request). Open commitments = committed but not yet obligated.
  • Obligation: a legally binding agreement to pay (contract award, travel order, payroll). The main measure of execution.
  • Disbursement (expenditure): cash actually paid out against an obligation.
  • Unobligated balance = AFP − obligations. Allotted but unobligated = allotment − obligations (money in hand not yet put on contract).
  • Obligation rate = obligations ÷ AFP. Plan rate = cumulative spend plan ÷ AFP. Variance = obligation rate − plan rate (points) or obligations − plan (dollars).
  • Burn rate = average monthly obligations over the last three months.
  • Projected end-of-year (EOY) execution = obligations to date + remaining plan × adjusted pace. Pace = obligations ÷ plan to date (bounded 50%–115%), moved partway back toward 100% because programs usually recover part of a shortfall, and trusted less early in the year. Working from the plan respects seasonal programs such as Annual Training pay and construction awards.
  • Use-or-lose risk: one-year money (NGPA, OMNG, and State in this demo) that is projected to finish the year below 97% obligated (and more than $25K short) with three or fewer months left. After September 30 the unobligated balance of one-year money expires. MCNG is multi-year money and is not flagged.
  • State funds are shown on the federal fiscal year here for comparison; a real state ledger may use a July–June year.
