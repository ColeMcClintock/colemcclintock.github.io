How the numbers are calculated
  • Obligation and Commitments are the export's cumulative amounts (obligation_amt_cum and commitment_amt_cum), added up for the lines in the current filters.
  • ALLOBJ lines (Commitment Item equal to ALLOBJ, any case or spacing) are removed when the file is read. They are never counted, charted, listed, exported, or saved.
  • Documents = distinct Document Numbers among the lines shown. A document can have several lines (one per Commitment Item).
  • Amounts accept commas, dollar signs, parentheses and trailing minus signs for negatives. Blank, #REF! and other error cells count as blank ($0 in totals).
  • Charts show the 10 largest values; the remainder is grouped as Other. A blank value is shown as (blank).
