How the numbers are calculated
  • Tech in the export is shown as MSC (major subordinate command, the level above battalion). A column named MSC is also accepted.
  • Open investigation = Status other than Final. Overdue = open and more than 90 days past the accident date (Due Date = Accident Date + 90). The export's Days Overdue is used when present; otherwise it is computed from the dates.
  • Costs: Injury Cost = days away + hospitalized + permanent partial + permanent total disability + fatality costs. Material ECOD = equipment and material damage; a blank ECOD counts as $0 in totals and as "no material damage" in counts.
  • Days lost uses the export's Days Lost column.
  • Fiscal year is the federal year, 1 October to 30 September (FY26 = Oct 2025 to Sep 2026).
  • Accidents per 1,000 (annualized) appears only when a Strength sheet is loaded: accidents ÷ assigned strength × 1,000 × 12 ÷ months in the date range.
  • Days since last Class A/B counts to the export date (demo) or today (your file).
  • Reported Via and Mistake Type are read but not charted. Gender, rank, age and years of service appear only as aggregated counts; groups under 3 records are suppressed, and they are left out of the record detail view and the summary.
