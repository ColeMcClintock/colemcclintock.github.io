How the numbers are calculated
  • Assigned strength = assigned ÷ authorized (sum across selected units).
  • MOS-qualified, individual & collective training are weighted by assigned strength.
  • Medically ready = (MRC 1 + MRC 2) ÷ assigned. MRC 1 Ready, MRC 2 Ready with minor gaps, MRC 3 Not ready (temporary), MRC 4 Not ready / unknown.
  • Equipment on-hand and operational readiness are weighted by authorized strength.
  • Overall rating (C1–C4) is a simplified illustrative method: each area (personnel, equipment, training) gets a level; the rating is the average level, never more than one level better than the weakest area. It is not the Army's official readiness reporting method.
  • Standards used for attention flags: strength 90%, MOSQ 85%, medical 85%, equipment on-hand 90%, equipment OR 85%, individual training 85%, collective training 80%.
